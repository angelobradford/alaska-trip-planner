# Bradford Family Kitchen

Meal planning and grocery list for a family of seven: high protein, low sugar, potato first, few ingredients, short hands-on time.

- **Week**: plans 7 dinners (optional breakfasts) from the built-in library following the starch mix (default 4 potato, 1 rice, 1 pasta, 1 grain). Swap, lock, or skip any night. Prep-day checklist gathers make-ahead steps.
- **Shop**: separate lists per store (Walmart and Costco to start; add more under Household). Each item remembers its store. A grocery list built from the week's meals (scaled to your servings) plus your staples, grouped by aisle. As-needed staples get a Need/Have check. When you finish a trip, each staple remembers what you actually bought (median of recent trips).
- **Recipes**: ~30 built-in recipes, favorites, hide, and add your own.
- **Household**: servings, kitchen limits (max ingredients, max hands-on minutes), starch mix, "kids won't eat" list, staples (with snack priority ★), and a paste-a-list importer.

`app.html` is the source. Run `./build.sh` to produce `index.html`, a standalone copy that saves to the browser on that device. The published claude.ai version shares data between everyone signed in.
